HOW IT WORKS

Getting your business online, one step at a time.

We aim to make the website process clear and straightforward. Here's what to expect when working with Pearl Web Studio.

Start with a conversation →

YOUR PROJECT JOURNEY

From first enquiry to launch

  1. 01

    Tell us about your project

    Share your business, goals and the type of website you need. If you're interested in an online store, tell us about your products and the features you have in mind.

  2. 02

    Choose a suitable package

    We'll help you compare options. E-commerce websites are priced at R7,500 - R8,500; payment gateway setup and provider fees may be additional.

    Compare website packages →
  3. 03

    Confirm the project

    We'll agree on the project details, scope and timeline. A 50% deposit is required before development begins.

  4. 04

    Design and development

    We create your website based on the agreed requirements and keep you informed as the work progresses.

  5. 05

    Review and agreed changes

    You'll have an opportunity to review the website and request changes covered by the agreed project scope.

  6. 06

    Final payment and launch

    After approval, the remaining 50% is due before final handover. We'll complete the agreed testing and deployment and provide relevant handover information.

GETTING READY

What to prepare for your project

Having some of these details ready can help us understand what you need.

  • Your business name and logo, if available
  • A short description of your business
  • Your services or products
  • Contact information
  • Images you'd like to use
  • Your preferred website colours and style
  • Any additional features you need

Don't have everything ready? That's okay. We can discuss what you need.
