BEFORE WORK BEGINS

Project terms

These points summarize the information currently published on this website. Confirm the details for your project in writing before making payment.

Project scope and changes

The selected package or written quote describes the agreed work. You can review the website and request changes covered by that agreed scope. Additional features or work outside the scope may be quoted separately.

Payment

A 50% deposit is required before development begins. The remaining 50% is payable before final website handover. Your project quote should confirm the price and payment details.

Timing

The project timeline is agreed before development begins. The schedule may depend on the project requirements and the materials or feedback needed from you.

Domains, hosting and third-party services

Domain registration, hosting, payment providers and other third-party services are separate unless your written quote says otherwise. Provider charges, subscriptions and transaction fees may also apply.

Cancellation and refunds

Cancellation and refund arrangements must be confirmed in writing for your project before you pay a deposit. Ask for the terms that apply before work starts, while work is in progress, and after completion. This page does not promise a refund.

For questions or to discuss a project, contact Pearl Web Studio.
