FAQs
1. How much does a website cost?

Our website packages start at R1,500 for a one-page website. The final price depends on the package and any additional features you require.

2. How long does it take to build a website?

The timeline depends on the size and complexity of the project. We'll provide an estimated completion date before development begins.

3. Do I need to purchase a domain?

A domain is your website's unique internet address. We can assist you with registering and connecting a domain to your website.

4. Do you offer website hosting?

Yes. We can assist with finding and setting up suitable hosting options depending on your website requirements.

5. Will my website work on mobile phones?

Yes. We aim to create responsive websites that adjust to different screen sizes, including smartphones, tablets and computers.

6. Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. We offer website redesign services to improve the appearance, layout and functionality of existing websites.

7. Do you offer website maintenance?

Yes. Our basic website maintenance service starts at R500 per month.

8. Can I request additional features?

Yes. Additional features such as booking systems, payment integrations and other plugins can be discussed and quoted separately.

9. How do payments work?

We require a 50% deposit before development begins. The remaining 50% is payable before the final website handover.

10. Can I update my website after it has been completed?

Yes, depending on the platform and website setup. We can also assist with updates through our maintenance service.

11. Do you work with startups and small businesses?

Absolutely! We work with individuals, entrepreneurs, startups and businesses looking to establish or improve their online presence.

12. How do I get started?

Simply contact us through WhatsApp or our enquiry form. Tell us about your project, and we'll guide you through the next steps.
