YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy notice

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This notice explains what happens when you browse Pearl Web Studio's website or contact us about a project.

Information you choose to provide

The project enquiry form asks for your name, email address, project type and message. Business name, budget and timing are optional. The form does not submit your details to a server. It prepares a message in WhatsApp, and the message is only shared with us if you review and send it there.

You can also contact us by phone, WhatsApp or email. Information you send through those services is handled by the relevant service provider as well as by us.

How we use enquiries

We use information you send to respond to your enquiry, discuss or provide requested services, and manage our communications with you. We do not sell your enquiry details.

Third-party services

The Contact page embeds Google Maps. Loading that map may share technical information, such as your device or browser details, with Google. WhatsApp and email services have their own privacy practices. Review their notices for details:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • WhatsApp Privacy Policy

Your choices and questions

Do not include sensitive personal information in a project enquiry unless it is necessary. For questions about information you have sent us or to request an update or deletion, email pearrlwebstudio@gmail.com.

If this website's enquiry process or third-party services change, this notice should be updated to reflect the change.
